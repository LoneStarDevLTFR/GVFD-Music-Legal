# GVFD Music Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026  
**Developer & Owner:** LoneStarDev

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy establishes how the GVFD Music Bot accesses, processes, retains, and protects information during normal application operation.

**The application processes information necessary for its intended functionality and operational security.**

## 2. Information Processed

The application may access and process the following information:

- Discord usernames and user identifiers.
- Discord server and channel identifiers.
- Voice channel connection information.
- Slash command interactions.
- Music search queries and requested URLs.
- Music playback queues.
- Technical diagnostics and application error logs.

The application does not intentionally collect personal passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information.

**The application is not designed to record, store, or transcribe users' voice conversations.**

## 3. Use of Information

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

- Executing music and playback commands.
- Managing voice channel connections.
- Maintaining music playback queues.
- Monitoring application health.
- Troubleshooting technical issues.
- Maintaining application security.
- Preventing unauthorized use and abuse.

**Discord API data is not sold, used for targeted advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.**

## 4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to support application functionality, maintain security, resolve technical issues, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Temporary operational information may be retained in application memory during active sessions.

Technical diagnostic information may be retained within restricted hosting logs.

Discord API data will be deleted when it is no longer necessary, upon a valid deletion request, or when required by applicable law or Discord policies, except where retention is legally required.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The application may utilize the following third-party services:

- **Discord** — Application connectivity and interactions.
- **YouTube** — Music searches and media retrieval.
- **PebbleHost** — Application hosting and infrastructure.

Music search queries, requested URLs, and necessary technical information may be transmitted to relevant service providers to fulfill user requests.

Third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

## 6. Information Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are maintained to protect application credentials and operational information.

Access to restricted application files, credentials, hosting configurations, and technical logs is limited to authorized personnel.

While reasonable security measures are maintained, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. User Rights & Data Deletion

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information associated with their use of the application.

Requests may be submitted directly to LoneStarDev through the Gran Vista Fire Department Discord support ticket system.

Reasonable identity verification may be required before processing a request.

## 8. Intellectual Property

**All original proprietary software, application systems, and development materials owned by LoneStarDev remain the intellectual property of LoneStarDev.**

This provision does not establish ownership over users' personal information, Discord API data, or third-party copyrighted materials.

Users retain their applicable privacy and data protection rights.

## 9. Age Requirements

Users must satisfy Discord's minimum age requirements and any applicable legal age requirements within their jurisdiction.

## 10. Policy Amendments

This Privacy Policy may be amended to reflect changes in application functionality, operational practices, platform policies, or applicable legal requirements.

Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate notice.

## 11. Contact

Privacy concerns, information requests, and data deletion requests may be directed to LoneStarDev through the Gran Vista Fire Department Discord support ticket system.
